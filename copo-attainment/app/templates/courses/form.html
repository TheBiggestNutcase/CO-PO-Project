Attainment targets

These four numbers decide how a Course Outcome's raw marks turn into a 0-3 attainment Level on the Results page. The first three set the scoring bands; the last one decides, for each student, whether they personally "cleared" a CO at all.

The minimum overall attainment % a CO needs to be scored as Level 1 at all. Below this, it's Level 0 - meaning the outcome wasn't attained. Commonly 50%.
The overall attainment % needed to move up to Level 2 - a solidly-met outcome. Should be higher than the Level 1 target. Commonly 60%.
The overall attainment % needed to reach Level 3, the highest band - the outcome was met very well. Should be higher than the Level 2 target. Commonly 70%.
A per-student bar, not a class-wide one: the % of marks a student must score on a CO's internal questions (IA tests + assignments/quiz/seminar) to count as having cleared that CO themselves. This feeds into the attainment % that the three Level targets above are then measured against. Commonly 60%.
